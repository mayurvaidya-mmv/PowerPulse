About PowerPulse

The Ultimate Smart Energy Monitoring Solution

What is PowerPulse?

PowerPulse is a comprehensive, industrial-grade energy dashboard designed to provide real-time visibility into your power infrastructure. Whether you are managing traditional utility grid power or auxiliary generator systems, PowerPulse consolidates complex three-phase data into intuitive, actionable insights. By leveraging modern IoT protocols and advanced AI algorithms, we help you detect anomalies, reduce operational downtime, and optimize energy costs.

Real-Time IoT

Instantaneous streaming of three-phase voltage, current, and power metrics via AWS IoT Core and MQTT protocols.

AI-Driven Analytics

Harness the power of AWS Bedrock and OpenAI to generate automated executive summaries and diagnostic reports.

Dynamic Alerting

Configurable multi-parameter threshold monitoring with instant notifications via AWS SNS and cooldown protection.

Downtime Tracking

Automatic logging of source switching events and downtime incidents to calculate precise system uptime percentages.

Technology Stack

PowerPulse is built upon a resilient, cloud-native architecture for maximum reliability.

Node.js / Express AWS IoT Core DynamoDB AWS SNS AWS Bedrock OpenAI GPT-4o Chart.js WebSockets (Socket.io)
